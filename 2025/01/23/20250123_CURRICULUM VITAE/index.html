CURRICULUM VITAE

2025.01.23

Author: Tianyu Fu

I am a PhD candidate at Tianjin University, China, and currently a CSC-funded visiting PhD researcher at Bangor University, United Kingdom, working with R. Iestyn Woolway. My research focuses on the development and evaluation of aquatic ecosystem models and numerical simulation of submerged macrophytes. I have developed the automatic calibration toolkit, EFDC-ACT, for aquatic ecosystem models (downloads exceeding 150).

Research Interest

My research interests are deeply rooted in aquatic ecosystems, with a focus on understanding and modeling hydrodynamic, environmental, and ecological interactions within aquatic ecosystems. I am currently engaged in elucidating the ecological roles of submerged macrophytes and developing aquatic ecosystem models to advance management strategies.
