CURRICULUM VITAE

2025.01.23TIANYU FU

Author: Tianyu Fu

I am a PhD Candidate at Tianjin University, China, with research focus including development and evaluation of aquatic ecosystem models, numerical simulation of submerged macrophytes. I have developed the automatic calibration toolkit, EFDC-ACT, for aquatic ecosystem models (downloads exceeding 150).

Research Interest

My research interests are deeply rooted in aquatic ecosystems, with a focus on understanding and modeling hydrodynamic, environmental, and ecological interactions within aquatic ecosystems. I am currently engaged in elucidating the ecological roles of submerged macrophytes and developing aquatic ecosystem models to advance management strategies.
