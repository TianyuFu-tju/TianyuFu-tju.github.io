About

I'm currently a PhD student in Tianjin University, China. My research interests are deeply rooted in aquatic ecosystems, with a focus on:

  1. Understanding complex hydrodynamic, environmental, and ecological interactions within aquatic ecosystems;
  2. Reproducing ecosystem dynamics through advanced numerical modeling;
  3. Applying these computational approaches to inform preservation and restoration strategies for vulnerable aquatic ecosystems.
