About me

Between nature and code,
there are questions to explore.

I am a PhD candidate at Tianjin University, China. My research focuses on developing and evaluating aquatic ecosystem models, including numerical simulations of submerged macrophytes.

I developed EFDC-ACT, an automatic calibration toolkit for aquatic ecosystem models. Here I share my research, tools, and ideas at the intersection of water, ecology, and code.
